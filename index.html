Research Settings

How automatic price search works.

Recommended source stack

Catalog identification: Numista (automatic, shown in Price Research). Prices: MA-Shops (automatic) + eBay (2 one-click links). For a serious valuation, keep active asking prices separate from real sold/auction prices.
Live search MA-Shops: runs through a small local backend (Python) on your computer — it has no CORS/proxy restrictions, so it's far more reliable than searching directly from the browser. Open START_COINBIDS.bat (leave its console window open while you use it) and let it run in the background. Without it, Collection/Wishlist/Sales still work normally — only live price search needs the backend.

Why not eBay automatically? eBay protects its search page with a JavaScript challenge ("Pardon Our Interruption") that requires a real browser to pass — no simple automated request reliably gets past it. eBay stays available as a one-click link ("eBay" / "eBay sold" in Price Research), which opens in your own browser and works normally.
